# ABSTRACT

SkillHer is an AI-powered skill development and learning recommendation platform designed to support individuals, particularly women, in identifying their strengths, addressing skill gaps, and making informed career decisions through personalized learning guidance. In today's competitive and technology-driven environment, many learners struggle to choose the right skills, courses, and career pathways due to the overwhelming amount of information available online and the lack of personalized support. SkillHer aims to bridge this gap by providing a centralized platform where users can assess their current skills, define career goals, and receive customized recommendations tailored to their individual needs and aspirations. The system enables users to create profiles, participate in skill assessments, track their learning progress, and access AI-generated recommendations that align with their interests and professional objectives. By analyzing user inputs and assessment results, the platform identifies areas for improvement and suggests relevant learning resources, skills, and development pathways that can enhance employability and career growth. The platform is developed using modern web technologies and follows a user-centric approach to ensure accessibility, simplicity, and effectiveness. Unlike conventional learning platforms that offer generic recommendations, SkillHer focuses on delivering personalized guidance that adapts to each user's profile and learning journey. The system also encourages continuous learning by helping users monitor their progress and stay motivated through structured recommendations and goal-oriented development plans. By integrating artificial intelligence with skill assessment and learning support, SkillHer creates an inclusive digital environment that empowers users to take control of their professional growth and lifelong learning journey. The proposed platform contributes to reducing skill mismatches, improving access to relevant educational opportunities, and supporting career advancement through intelligent recommendation mechanisms. Ultimately, SkillHer serves as a practical solution for promoting self-improvement, confidence, and sustainable career development in an increasingly dynamic and evolving workforce.

**Keywords:** Artificial Intelligence, Skill Development, Personalized Learning, Learning Recommendation System, Career Growth, Skill Assessment, Educational Technology, Career Guidance, User Profiling, SkillHer.
