# Paper Selection Criteria for SkillHer Literature Review

This document outlines the strict criteria used to select academic papers for the SkillHer literature review. All papers must meet these requirements to be considered for inclusion.

## Publication Requirements

### 1. Publication Year
- **Must be published between 2023–2026** (inclusive)
- Papers from 2022 or earlier are automatically excluded
- Papers from 2027 or later are not yet available for consideration

### 2. Approved Publishers
Papers must be published by one of the following publishers:
- **IEEE** (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)
- **ACM** (Association for Computing Machinery)
- **Springer** (including Springer Nature imprints)
- **Elsevier** (including ScienceDirect publications)
- **MDPI** (Multidisciplinary Digital Publishing Institute)
- **Taylor & Francis**
- **Frontiers** (Frontiers in journals)
- **Inderscience** (Inderscience Publishers)

## Relevance Requirements

Papers must demonstrate clear relevance to at least one of the following SkillHer domains:

### Primary Focus Areas (Must address at least one)
1. **AI Career Guidance**
   - Artificial intelligence applications in career counseling
   - Machine learning for career path prediction
   - AI-driven job matching and recommendation systems
   - Virtual career advisors or chatbots for professional development

2. **Personalized Learning Systems**
   - Adaptive learning technologies
   - Learning path customization based on user profiles
   - Intelligent tutoring systems
   - Competency-based learning frameworks

3. **Skill Gap Analysis**
   - Methods for identifying skill deficiencies
   - Competency mapping and assessment techniques
   - Future skills forecasting
   - Skill inventory and gap measurement tools

4. **Learning Analytics**
   - Educational data mining
   - Learning behavior analysis and prediction
   - Performance tracking and feedback systems
   - Predictive modeling for learning outcomes

### Secondary Focus Areas (Must address at least one if primary not present)
5. **Women Empowerment through Technology**
   - Technology interventions for gender equality in workforce
   - Digital inclusion initiatives for women
   - Tech-based solutions addressing gender-specific career barriers
   - Studies on women's participation in STEM fields

6. **Generative AI in Education**
   - Applications of LLMs (Large Language Models) in learning
   - Generative AI for educational content creation
   - AI-assisted tutoring and feedback systems
   - Ethical considerations of generative AI in educational contexts

7. **Career Recommendation Systems**
   - Recommendation algorithms for job/career paths
   - Collaborative filtering in career contexts
   - Content-based career recommendation systems
   - Hybrid recommendation approaches for career development

## Verification Requirements

### Mandatory Verification for Each Paper
- [ ] **Title Verification**: Confirm title matches official publication
- [ ] **Author Verification**: Confirm authors are real individuals with institutional affiliations
- [ ] **Publisher Verification**: Confirm publisher is on approved list
- [ ] **Year Verification**: Confirm publication year is 2023-2026
- [ ] **DOI Verification**: Confirm DOI exists and resolves to correct paper
- [ ] **URL Verification**: Confirm official URL is accessible and legitimate
- [ ] **Relevance Assessment**: Determine relevance score (1-10) based on alignment with SkillHer domains

## Exclusion Criteria

Papers will be excluded if they:
- ❌ Published before 2023 or after 2026
- ❌ From non-approved publishers (e.g., predatory journals, unverified sources)
- ❌ Lack verifiable DOI or official URL
- ❌ Have fictional or unverifiable author information
- ❌ Show no relevance to any SkillHer domain (score < 3/10)
- ❌ Are duplicates of already selected papers
- ❌ Are retracted or have expressions of concern without resolution
- ❌ Are only available as abstracts without full text access
- ❌ Are non-peer-reviewed preprints without subsequent journal publication (unless from verified repositories with DOI)

## Scoring Guidelines

### Relevance Score (1-10)
- **9-10**: Directly addresses core SkillHer components with significant methodological or theoretical contributions
- **7-8**: Strongly relevant to SkillHer domains with clear applicability
- **5-6**: Moderately relevant with some applicable insights or methodologies
- **3-4**: Minimally relevant with tangential connections
- **1-2**: Barely relevant or not relevant to SkillHer objectives

## Selection Process Workflow

1. **Initial Search**: Search approved publisher databases using domain-specific keywords
2. **Screening**: Apply year and publisher filters to initial results
3. **Verification**: Verify DOI, URL, title, authors, and publisher for each candidate
4. **Relevance Scoring**: Assign relevance score based on alignment with SkillHer domains
5. **Ranking**: Sort papers by relevance score (highest first)
6. **Final Selection**: Select top 10 papers that meet all criteria
7. **Documentation**: Complete PAPER_COLLECTION.md and FINAL_SELECTED_PAPERS.md templates

## Quality Assurance

- Two independent verifiers recommended for critical papers
- All DOIs must be tested using https://doi.org/
- Publisher verification via official website cross-check
- Year verification via copyright or publication date in paper
- Relevance scoring should include brief justification
